How to read this board: each call (BUY / HOLD / WATCH / SELL) is generated from price trend, recent momentum, how often the counter actually trades, and whether outstanding orders lean toward buyers or sellers — never from a single number. Most DSE counters trade thinly, so a lot of counters honestly land on WATCH until more daily reports have been ingested.
